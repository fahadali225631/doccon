Why Use a Client-Side Document Converter?

Traditional file converters upload your sensitive files to external servers, risking data leaks. DocCon processes your document directly within your web browser. Your data never leaves your smartphone or computer, offering 100% data privacy.

How to Format Word Documents for Flawless PDF Output

To ensure perfect alignments when printing your Word documents to PDF, stick to standard global web fonts such as Arial, Calibri, or Times New Roman. Complex overlapping graphic vector layers should be flattened inside your Word processor before running the conversion.
