How to Convert Files to PDF

Welcome to DocCon, your reliable, secure, and fast solution for document and image conversions. Whether you need to turn a text document into a shareable file or compile a photo gallery into a secure document format, our platform processes everything directly within your browser ensuring absolute privacy.

  • Step 1: Select your preferred conversion mode from the top menu (Word to PDF or Image to PDF).
  • Step 2: Click the upload area to select your files. For Word, we support standard .docx formats. For images, you can select multiple JPG, PNG, or WebP files at once.
  • Step 3: Preview your content to ensure accuracy. Click "Download as PDF" to instantly generate your finalized file.

Why Choose DocCon?

Our platform is built on a client-side architecture. This means your files are never uploaded to remote servers. All text processing and image formatting happens utilizing your device's memory, ensuring zero data-logging and maximum speed.

  • Smart Image Sizing: Unlike traditional converters that leave awkward white borders, our image engine automatically calculates the dimensions of your photos to stretch edge-to-edge.
  • Style Preservation: We utilize an advanced document rendering system that reads your headers, colors, fonts, and layouts to ensure your document looks exactly the way you authored it.
